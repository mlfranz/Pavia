DRAFT:
As a purely computational project, the largest environmental footprint
is compute usage (PBPK simulation runs and generative-model/surrogate
training) and international travel between the outgoing and return
hosts. The project will mitigate this by: (1) reusing the approximately
1,979 pool-scale PBPK simulations already generated during the
preliminary DAAD-funded audit rather than re-running them, and by
using a trainable surrogate specifically to avoid full PBPK simulation
inside the generative optimization loop (Section 1.2/3.1), reducing
compute relative to a naive full-simulation approach; (2) preferring
remote collaboration between the outgoing and return hosts for routine
supervision and coordination, reserving in-person travel for the
mobility transitions themselves (outgoing-to-return phase) and events
where in-person participation is materially valuable (e.g., conference
presentations); (3) following both institutions' own sustainability/
green-lab practices where applicable.
